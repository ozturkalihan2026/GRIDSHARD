Built-in imagegen; edit target: client/assets/ui/cores-physical-v1/core_resonance.webp.

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset: GRIDSHARD destroyed reactor battle sprite, transparent PNG. Image 1 is the edit target (cyan resonance reactor). Change ONLY this reactor into its destroyed, powered-off wreck AFTER its explosion. Preserve exactly the original casual painted mobile-game art language, chunky hard-edged plates, dark outlines, camera angle, cyan/silver/gold identity and lower pedestal footprint. Keep the pedestal grounded in the SAME lower position and same width. Break the circular upper housing into 2-3 attached bent sections, shatter/remove the central glowing crystal leaving a dark empty socket and a few dull cyan fragments resting within the pedestal; scorch the broken edges. Result is a compact recognizable wreck, not a faded intact reactor. Upper silhouette should be lower than the intact sprite, with meaningful empty transparent space above. No active light, no halo, no flames, no smoke, no floating particles, no shadow outside the object, no extra scene, no text, no border. Do not change camera or crop the base. Genuine transparent background, clean readable sprite.
